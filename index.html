What the session covers

Five things most investors are never told plainly.

Structure first, outcomes later. Each segment explains a mechanism and the conditions under which it does and does not apply.

01

Why this asset class draws high-income capital

How non-operated working interests differ from royalties, funds, and energy equities — and why the tax treatment of a direct working interest is the reason the structure exists at all.

02

Where the deductions come from

Intangible drilling costs, tangible costs, and depletion explained at the mechanism level: what each one is, what it attaches to, and why the headline percentages people quote at dinner parties are usually missing their conditions.

03

Why timing decides the outcome

How the placed-in-service and year-end calendar interacts with when capital is committed, and why the same investment made eight weeks apart can be treated very differently.

04

The risks, stated directly

Dry holes, commodity price exposure, illiquidity, operator quality, capital calls, and the difference between a potential tax benefit and a guaranteed one. This segment is not an afterthought — it is the reason the session exists.

05

The questions to ask before you ever write a check

A short diligence framework you can take to any sponsor, including this one, plus how to raise the subject with your CPA so the conversation is forward-looking rather than a post-mortem.

Suitability

Not everyone qualifies. That is by design.

There is a real qualification process and a minimum investment. It is more useful to know early whether this is irrelevant to you.

Likely a fit if

  • You are an accredited investor, or expect to qualify
  • You earn a high income and consistently pay substantial federal tax
  • You work with a CPA but suspect proactive planning has not been explored
  • You prefer to understand structures and tradeoffs before considering anything
  • You are comfortable with complexity, risk, and long-term decisions

Not a fit if

  • You are seeking guarantees, projections, or specific outcomes
  • You are looking for quick wins or short-term solutions
  • You are uncomfortable with alternative assets or illiquidity
  • You prefer to delegate all thinking and decision-making to someone else
  • You are not accredited, or unwilling to have a suitability conversation

Questions

Before you register.

Who is this session for?

Accredited investors and high-income professionals who consistently pay substantial federal tax and want to understand how oil and gas tax structures work before deciding whether they are relevant. It is educational, not an offer.

Does attending commit me to anything?

No. Nothing is offered or sold on the session, and there is no obligation of any kind. Attendees who want to go further can request a suitability conversation afterwards; most will not, and that is a legitimate outcome.

Are oil and gas investments suitable for everyone?

No. All investing carries risk, and these are private, illiquid, long-horizon investments that are unsuitable for many people regardless of income. Potential tax treatment depends on the specific structure and on your individual circumstances. The session covers these limits directly.

Will specific returns or tax savings be presented?

No. No projections, guaranteed savings, or performance figures are presented. The session explains how structures are designed to work and what determines whether a benefit applies at all.

Is it live or recorded?

Each session is presented live and includes live Q&A. Registrants who cannot attend receive access to a replay.

Why has my CPA never raised this?

Most CPAs are engaged to report accurately on what already happened rather than to explore alternative structures ahead of time. Oil and gas sits where tax law, energy markets, and private investing overlap, which falls outside many practices’ scope.

What happens after the session?

You receive the replay and a short summary. If you want to explore whether the strategy applies to your situation, you can request a one-to-one suitability call. There is no follow-up pressure either way.

Understand it properly, then decide.

Sixty minutes of plain explanation — structures, timing, and risk — so the decision is yours to make calmly, with or without us.
