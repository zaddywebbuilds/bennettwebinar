Rules that apply across every path

The guardrails.

Rule 01

Booking stops everything

A booked suitability call cancels every active nurture sequence instantly. No exceptions.

Rule 02

Every sequence ends

Each path has a final message that says it is the final message. Nothing drips indefinitely.

Rule 03

Consent is per-purpose

Session reminders and ongoing educational email are separate permissions, captured separately at registration.

Rule 04

No fabricated urgency

No countdown timers, no invented seat counts, no expiring replays. The year-end calendar is the only real deadline and it is stated plainly.

Rule 05

No claims in email

No projections, savings figures, or performance numbers appear in any message. The disclosures travel with the educational content.

Rule 06

One unsubscribe ends all

Opting out removes the contact from every sequence at once, not just the one they received.
